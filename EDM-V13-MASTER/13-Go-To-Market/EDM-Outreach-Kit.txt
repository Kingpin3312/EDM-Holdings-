EDM HOLDINGS — OUTREACH KIT
The five messages that do the actual selling. Fill the brackets, send
from your own address, plain text, no images. Short beats clever every
time — commercial managers read these on their phones between meetings.
Attach the capability statement to the first touch and nothing else.

A NOTE ON RHYTHM
------------------------------------------------------------
First touch, then one follow-up, then leave it a month. Two messages is
persistence; four is a nuisance. The fastest way onto a tender list is
still a name — if anyone at EDM knows anyone at the contractor, that
introduction beats both emails put together.
