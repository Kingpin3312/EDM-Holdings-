1. "Markets  UAE · Ireland · UK · Australia"
   Decision 3: delivery is the UAE, through EDM Technical Services L.L.C. The
   UK, Ireland and Australia are the team's track record and separate companies.
   A structured "Markets" field is exactly the kind of statement Decision 3
   restricts to Dubai. This is the document that goes to a Tier-1 procurement
   team, so it is the worst place for the claim to survive.

   reads as an incorporation date.
