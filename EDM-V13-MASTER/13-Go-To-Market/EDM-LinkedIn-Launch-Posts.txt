POST 2 — WHY SELF-DELIVERY (three to five days later)
------------------------------------------------------------
Every time an interior package gets split across subcontractors, someone
inherits the joints between them.

Programme slips in the gaps. Quality arguments happen at the interfaces.
And when something needs fixing, three firms point at each other.

That's why we self-deliver. Our own people, across every trade inside the
building — so when a main contractor hands us the package, the interfaces
are ours to manage, not theirs to referee.

It's not a complicated idea. It's just rare to see it done properly.
