EDM ILLUSTRATION SUITE
======================
Original drawn artwork for use anywhere the
brand needs a visual before real project photography exists. Nothing
here pretends to be a photo of our work — that honesty is the point.

edm-hero-interior.svg   The signature piece: an isometric room mid-fit-out —
                        exposed studs in green, one bay boarded in the cube
                        greys, a taped wall and ceiling grid. The logo itself never appears in artwork — the approved files in this folder are the only mark.
                        Used on the website share image and the online
                        capability statement cover.
edm-sector-*.svg        Four matching motifs — residential, workplace,
                        hotels, education — for sector pages, decks or posts.
edm-pattern-cubes.svg   A quiet repeating cube tile for banners and dividers.

All transparent-background SVGs in the locked palette only (white, green
#083819, cube greys #7C8282/#9EA3A2, ink, hairline). Scale to any size.
When real photography arrives, it takes priority; this suite stays for
diagrams, social and anywhere a camera can't go.
