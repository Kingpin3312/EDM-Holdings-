V12 CORRECTIONS APPLIED
-----------------------
The retired bone tints (#F5F2EA and variants) were the dominant ground of the
workwear and livery renders. Removed — the ground is now #FFFFFF.

The vehicle livery used #0E3A2C for the wrap, not #083819, and its swatch bar
printed "EMERALD · #0E3A2C" and "IVORY · #F5F2EA" as if those were the brand
palette. Both the fill and the swatch bar are corrected. The bar now reads
EMERALD #083819 / WHITE #FFFFFF / STEEL #9AA3A0.

This mattered because these are the two files most likely to be handed to a
signwriter or a workwear supplier as a colour reference. In V11 that would have
put the retired palette into physical production.
