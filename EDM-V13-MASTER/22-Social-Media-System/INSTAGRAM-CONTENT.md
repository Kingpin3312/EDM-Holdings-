# INSTAGRAM CONTENT BANK

Thirty captions, six carousel scripts and six reel scripts. Written for
Instagram, not adapted from LinkedIn — shorter lines, the point in the first
sentence, no corporate register.

Everything here is verifiable from the pack. No client, project or main
contractor is named; consent is verbal only. Nothing invented.

**Caption structure:** first line does the work, because that is all anyone sees
before "more". Then two or three short lines. Then hashtags, three to five.

## CAROUSEL SCRIPTS — 6

Use `IG-carousel-01-cover`, `-02-body` (repeat for each step) and `-05-end`.

**CAR-1 · Five details that decide whether a package is easy or expensive**
1. Cover — as above. *None of them are glamorous. All are decided before anyone starts boarding.*
2. Setting out — from a survey, not the drawing.
3. Head-of-wall — the slab moves, the wall doesn't.
4. Fire-stopping — right product, right gap, tested arrangement, photographed.
5. Risers — buildable from one side, coordinated before framing.
6. Acoustic continuity — a wall performs at its junctions.
7. End card — *Send the drawings and the programme.*

**CAR-2 · What actually happens to a wall on our sites**
Cover → eight inspection stages, one per slide, in order → end card.

**CAR-3 · Cat A vs Cat B, in sixty seconds**
Cover → what Cat A is → what Cat B is → where the seam costs money → what we do about it → end.

**CAR-4 · Why we employ our people**
Cover → what agency labour costs you in quality → known outputs → a standard that holds → answers from someone who was there → end card, hiring.

**CAR-5 · What's in a handover pack**
Cover → every wall numbered → every stage signed → photographed before it's covered → handed over per zone → end.

**CAR-6 · Five things a main contractor actually wants from a subcontractor**
Cover → turn up with the labour you promised → hold the sequence → produce the documentation without three reminders → flag risk early → leave the area clean → end.
