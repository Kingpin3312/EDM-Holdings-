# EDM HOLDINGS — SOCIAL MEDIA PLAYBOOK

**Version 1.0 · August 2026 · Owner: Christopher Simon**

This sits alongside `13-Go-To-Market/LinkedIn/EDM-Holdings-LinkedIn-Master-System.md`,
which holds the LinkedIn strategy, the 90-day calendar and 50 written posts. That
document is not replaced. This one adds Instagram, the visual template system,
and the rules for running both channels without them turning into the same feed.

---

## 1. THE ARGUMENT — WHY THE TWO CHANNELS ARE NOT THE SAME JOB

Most construction firms run one social strategy and post it twice. It fails on
Instagram, because the audience there is not the audience buying a fit-out
package.

**LinkedIn is the primary lead generation channel.** Not one of two — the one.
Commercial managers, procurement, project directors and consultants are all
there, and they are building a mental shortlist of firms who won't cause them a
problem. Content demonstrates competence: interfaces, programme, fire detail,
inspection records. Every enquiry target in this system comes from LinkedIn.

Budget the effort accordingly. If a week only allows one channel to be done
properly, it is LinkedIn, every time. Instagram can go quiet for a fortnight
without cost; LinkedIn cannot.

**Instagram does three different jobs.**

*One — recruitment.* Fixers, tapers, ceiling hands and supervisors do not read
LinkedIn. They are on Instagram, and in a self-delivery business the workforce
is the product. This is the highest commercial return Instagram will give you,
and almost no UAE subcontractor is using it properly.

*Two — the craft.* A framed floor, a clean head detail, a benchmark panel.
Instagram rewards the visual quality of the trade in a way LinkedIn does not.
It is where you look like people who care about the work.

*Three — the search.* A commercial manager who meets Christopher will look the
firm up. If the Instagram is dead, or it is fourteen Eid graphics and a stock
photo of a handshake, that is a data point about the business. If it is
disciplined, current and clearly the same firm as the website, that is a
different data point.

**What Instagram is not:** a lead channel. Do not measure it on enquiries. If it
produces one good supervisor hire in ninety days it has paid for itself several
times over.

## 2. THE CONSTRAINT WE ARE DESIGNING AROUND

**There is no site photography yet.** The pack says so; the brand system relies
on drawn illustration for that reason.

A social system that depends on photographs you do not have is a system that
runs for two weeks and stops. So the template suite is **type-led on white**.
Every template works with no photograph at all, and every one has a photographic
variant for when the images arrive.

This is not a compromise. In a feed of orange hi-vis and drone shots, a white
type-led grid is the most distinctive thing a UAE contractor could post. It also
happens to be exactly the brand.

**When photography does land** (see the brief in `09-Document-Sources`), shift
the ratio to roughly 60% photographic, 40% type. Not 100% — the type cards are
what make the grid look considered rather than like a camera roll.

## 3. THE TEMPLATE SUITE

Everything in `templates/` is generated by `make-templates.py` from the brand
tokens and the master cube artwork. **Do not open the PNGs and retype the text.**
Edit the content block at the foot of the script and re-run it. That is what
stops these drifting the way the workwear and livery renders did.

| Template | Size | Use |
|---|---|---|
| `LI-company-banner-1128x191` | 1128 × 191 | LinkedIn company page cover |
| `LI-personal-banner-1584x396` | 1584 × 396 | All four leadership profiles |
| `LI-link-card-1200x627` | 1200 × 627 | LinkedIn link previews and document covers |
| `avatar-400x400` | 400 × 400 | LinkedIn page and profile marks |
| `IG-avatar-320x320` | 320 × 320 | Instagram profile picture |
| `IG-square-statement` | 1080 × 1080 | A single idea, stated plainly |
| `IG-square-stat` | 1080 × 1080 | One number you can evidence |
| `IG-portrait-statement` | 1080 × 1350 | Same, with more feed height — the default |
| `IG-portrait-photo` | 1080 × 1350 | Photo above, caption beneath |
| `IG-carousel-01/02/05` | 1080 × 1350 | Cover, body and end-card grid |
| `IG-story-hiring` / `-progress` | 1080 × 1920 | Stories and Reel covers |
| `IG-highlight-*` | 1080 × 1920 | Five highlight covers |

**Format rules.** Portrait 1080 × 1350 takes more feed height than square and is
the default for anything you want seen. Carousels get more time-on-post than any
other static format — use them for anything with steps. Stories are for the
same day only; anything worth keeping goes to a highlight.

**Story safe zones** are marked on the template. Nothing important in the top
250 px or the bottom 300 px — the platform UI covers both.

## 5. CADENCE

The UAE working week is Monday to Friday. Nothing is scheduled Saturday or
Sunday, and nothing commercial goes out on National Day.

| | Monday | Tuesday | Wednesday | Thursday |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **LinkedIn** | Leadership post, 11:00 | Company post, 08:30 | Personal post, 11:00 | Company post, 08:30 |
| **Instagram** | — | Feed post, 12:30 | Story set | Feed post, 12:30 |

Two Instagram feed posts a week. Not more. A construction account posting daily
runs out of real material inside a month and starts posting filler, and filler
is worse than silence.

**Stories:** two or three a week, same-day, unpolished. A wall going up, a
delivery landing, a benchmark being signed. Stories are the one place the
production value can drop, and should.

**Reels:** one a fortnight. See section 7.

**Cross-posting:** never automatic. A LinkedIn post pasted into Instagram reads
as a memo. Section 6 of the Instagram content file has the same ideas rewritten
for the platform.

## 6. WHAT TO POST — THE PILLARS

Instagram runs five pillars, weighted differently from LinkedIn.

| Pillar | Share | What it looks like |
|---|---|---|
| **The craft** | 30% | Details, junctions, finishes, before and after from the same spot |
| **Recruitment** | 25% | Roles, what we offer, crews working. The commercial engine here |
| **The trade explained** | 20% | Carousels. What a shaft wall is, why fire-stopping matters |
| **People** | 15% | Supervisors, crews, the site cabin |
| **Company** | 10% | Milestones, documents, hiring pushes |

Note there is no "business development" pillar. On Instagram it converts nothing
and costs credibility. The CTA is still on every asset — it sits in the artwork
footer, as it does everywhere else — but the captions do not ask. See
`CTA-SYSTEM.md`.

**On LinkedIn the balance is different**, because that is the lead channel.
Business development is still only 3% of posts, but the CTA is heavier in the
furniture: the page button points at the capability statement, all four
leadership Abouts close with it, and every banner carries it.

## 7. REELS — THREE FORMATS, NOTHING ELSE

**The 20-second build.** One process, sped up. Studs going in, board going up,
tape and joint. Shot on a phone on a tripod or propped on a stack of board. No
music with lyrics, no countdown, no drone.

**The 45-second detail.** Kenny or a supervisor in front of an actual detail,
explaining what it is and why it is built that way. This is the highest-value
video EDM can make and the hardest thing for a competitor to copy, because it
requires someone who genuinely knows.

**The 30-second walk.** Supervisor walks a zone in portrait, talking. What is
built, what is next, one thing that was awkward.

**Every reel gets subtitles.** Most are watched with the sound off. **No
trending audio.** A serious contractor using a viral sound reads as a firm
trying to be something it isn't.

## 7a. THE CTA — ON EVERY ASSET

One ask, the same words everywhere: **Send the drawings and the programme.**

It appears on 100% of assets and in roughly 10% of captions. The full system —
the five approved variants, where each one sits, the banned closers and the
rules for writing so it does not read as generated — is in `CTA-SYSTEM.md`.
Read it before writing anything.

The short version: the CTA belongs in the furniture, not the message. A footer
line on the artwork is present when someone is ready to act and costs nothing
when they are not. "Contact us today" in the copy is asking, and asking on every
post is how an account stops being read by the people who buy.

## 8. WHAT WE NEVER POST

- Stock photography of any kind. Handshakes, hard hats, glowing city skylines.
- A client, project or main contractor name without written consent on file.
- Any client drawing, badge, screen, security arrangement or document.
- A person without correct PPE, or without their permission.
- An untidy area, a trip hazard, poorly stacked material. If it would fail a
  safety audit it fails as content.
- Motivational quotes. Sunset site photos with text over them.
- Eid, National Day and Ramadan graphics beyond one short, plain acknowledgement.
- More than five hashtags. Three is usually right.
- Anything that reads as generated rather than written.

## 10. MEASUREMENT

Instagram is not measured on enquiries. Track four things monthly:

| Measure | 90-day target | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Followers | 300+ | Below ~150 the account looks abandoned |
| Applications or DMs about work | 8+ | The actual return on this channel |
| Saves and shares per post | rising | Saves mean the content was useful, not just liked |
| Profile visits after a meeting week | — | Watch whether people look you up after you meet them |

Likes are not a measure. Reach is barely one. Saves, shares and messages are.

**Cross-channel:** log any Instagram-originated conversation in EDM OS with
Source = Instagram, using the fields already added in section 15 of the LinkedIn
master system.

## 12. THE FIRST FOUR WEEKS

**Week 1.** Create the account. Bio, avatar, five highlight covers, contact
details. Post the three launch pieces from the content file. Follow 30 relevant
accounts — main contractors, developers, trade suppliers, UAE construction media.

**Week 2.** Two feed posts, first story set. Start the recruitment push; it is
the fastest thing to show a result.

**Week 3.** First carousel. First reel if anybody can be filmed in front of a
detail.

**Week 4.** Review. Which format got saved, not which got liked. Rebuild the
next month's plan around it.

Do not buy followers. Do not use engagement pods. A UAE subcontractor with
40,000 followers and no comments is a firm nobody trusts with a package.
