EDM MEASURE — drywall takeoff
=============================

What it is
----------
The highlighter pen, replaced. Open a drawing, set the scale once, trace
your wall runs — and every trace lands in a live, priced bill of
quantities. No re-typing, no scaling off by eye, and every figure in the
bill links back to a line you can see on the drawing.

How to use it
-------------
1. Open index.html in any modern browser (Chrome, Edge, Safari).
   Nothing to install. Works on a laptop or a tablet.
2. Load drawing — PDF or a PNG/JPG photo/export of the layout.
3. Calibrate (C): click both ends of a known dimension, type its length.
4. Check (K): measure a second known dimension to prove the scale.
   Thirty seconds here is what makes everything after it accurate.
5. Trace (T): click along a wall run. Shift keeps it straight.
   Enter or double-click to finish, then set type, height, doors, zone.
6. Watch the Bill tab. Tap a row to light up its walls on the drawing.
   Export CSV drops straight into the pricing workbook.

Also in the box
---------------
- Wall type library with real build-ups (single/double layer, acoustic,
  shaftwall, linings) and editable supply and labour rates.
- Revision overlay: load rev C over rev B, tick Difference, and what
  changed shows up — measure the variation before anyone argues about it.
- Save project: one file holds the drawing, scale, runs and rates.
- Measurement register: every run, with a jump-to-drawing button.

Worth knowing
-------------
- Opening a PDF needs an internet connection the first time (the PDF
  reader loads from the web). Offline on site? Use a PNG or JPG export.
- Door deduction is 0.9 x 2.1 per opening. Rates ship with sensible
  defaults — put your own in before you price anything real.
- This is the standalone field tool. The same module is designed to sit
  inside EDM OS, where a takeoff becomes a tender line automatically.
