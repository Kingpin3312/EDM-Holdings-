EDM DAYBOOK — daily site record
===============================

What it is
----------
One page a day, kept on the site manager's phone, the day it happened.
Labour by trade, weather, deliveries, snags — and the section that pays
for itself: every instruction received, with who said it, what they said,
a photo if there's something to see, and the time it was said. Paper
forgets and WhatsApp scrolls away. This doesn't.

How to use it
-------------
1. Open index.html on your phone. The date is already today.
2. Log the gang in the morning, add the rest as the day happens.
3. When anyone tells you to do something beyond the drawings — the
   agent, the architect, anyone — log it there and then. Ten seconds.
   That entry is what the QS turns into a paid variation.
4. Save keeps every day so far, photos included, in one file. Send it
   to the QS at month-end or whenever they ask.
5. Print day produces the branded daily record — use the browser's
   "Save as PDF" to issue or file it.

The small print, in plain English
---------------------------------
- The printed record states plainly that instructions listed are notice
  of change, with formal confirmation to follow commercially. That line
  protects you both ways.
- Works fully offline. Nothing leaves the phone until you share the file.
- Pairs with the wall reference plan: in EDM Measure, "Print wall plan"
  gives you the laminated sheet for the cabin, so the refs the lads see
  are the refs in every record.
