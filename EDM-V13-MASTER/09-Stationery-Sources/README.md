## Brand specs (for your printer)

- **Typeface:** Montserrat (embedded in every file).
- **Colours:** Emerald `#083819` (C90 M55 Y72 K65 approx / suggest Pantone 5535 C),
  Ink `#0f231b`, Muted `#5c6f66`, White. No gold, wordmark only — no logo mark.
- **Business cards:** trim 90×54mm, **3mm bleed included** (files are 96×60mm),
  full-bleed emerald reverse. Recommend 350–400gsm uncoated/soft-touch stock.
- **A4 pieces:** designed within safe margins — print on any office printer or
  send to a print house. Uncoated 100–120gsm for letterhead reads premium.
