EDM CASHFLOW — applications, retention & cash
=============================================

What it is
----------
The board for the money nobody's system tracks: the gap between what we
apply for, what gets certified, and what actually lands. Applied is not
certified, and certified is not paid — this keeps eyes on all three, plus
the retention sitting in clients' accounts waiting to be chased home.

How to use it
-------------
1. Open index.html in any modern browser. Add each project once —
   retention percentage and payment terms live on the project.
2. One row per monthly application. Enter what was applied; when the
   certificate lands, enter what was certified. The shortfall works
   itself out — and if there's a shortfall with no reason logged, the
   page says so in red. An unexplained shortfall becomes a discount.
3. Due dates fill in from the payment terms; put the paid date in when
   the money arrives. Anything past due shows on the Board — that column
   is the early warning that a client is wobbling. The firms that
   collapsed took their subcontractors' unpaid certificates with them.
4. Retention tab: set practical completion and the defects period once.
   When a release date passes unclaimed, it says OVERDUE — CHASE IT,
   because across this industry the second half is routinely forgotten.
5. Print cash report gives the one-page monthly position for the board:
   totals, by-project table, cash due in the next 90 days, retention to
   recover. Browser "Save as PDF" to file or circulate.

Before the numbers go upstairs
------------------------------
- Figures here are management numbers for chasing cash, not the books —
  Xero stays the record of account. This is the layer Xero can't see.
- Works offline. One save file holds the whole position.
