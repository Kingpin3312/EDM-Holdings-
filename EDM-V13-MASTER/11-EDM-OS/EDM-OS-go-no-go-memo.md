# EDM OS — Deployment Go / No-Go

**To:** Board of Directors / Partners, EDM Holdings
**Date:** 27 June 2026
**Re:** Whether to deploy EDM OS as the company's operating platform

---

## Recommendation

- **As EDM's company-wide operating platform — do not deploy yet.**
- **As a limited pilot of the commercial modules on one live project — proceed now.**

## The situation

EDM OS is a well-built prototype. Its commercial front office — CRM, bid pipeline, estimator workload, account intelligence and document control — is strong, properly designed and ready to be tested. But it has never run end to end with real data or users, and the modules that would make it an operating platform for labour and site are not yet built.

## The gap that decides it

For a labour-only subcontractor, the core of the business is running men on site: labour allocation, daily attendance, timesheets, supervisor and foreman reporting, and productivity by trade. In EDM OS today these are navigation headings, not working systems. The platform currently helps us **win and administer** work; it does not yet help us **run the labour** — which is the heart of what EDM does. As the company's system of record, it is therefore incomplete.

## Why not now

- The operations core — labour allocation, attendance, timesheets, supervisor reporting, productivity — is not built.
- No offline mobile capability, and site attendance is inherently a phone-in-hand, poor-signal task.
- It has never been deployed or run as a whole; all data is sample data.
- Production security is not hardened (authentication, MFA, encrypted credentials), and it has had no security review or ISO alignment.

## What is ready

The commercial slice is genuinely good and worth putting in front of a real project now — running in parallel with existing spreadsheets, at low risk. This proves whether the team actually adopts it and surfaces what to fix, turning a prototype into evidence before further investment is committed.

## The path

1. **Pilot the commercial modules** on one project, three to four users, 30 days — low cost, proves adoption.
2. **Build the labour and site-operations core** — the real work, measured in months, not a finishing touch.
3. **Add an offline field capability** for supervisors.
4. **Harden security** (MFA, encryption), commission a review, and begin ISO alignment.
5. **Integrate HR, visa and payroll** rather than rebuild them.
6. **Deploy company-wide** only once the operations core is built and the pilot has proven use.

## Bottom line

Approve a pilot of the commercial platform now. Do not present EDM OS as the company-wide operating system to clients, ISO auditors or investors until the labour and site core is built, hardened and proven. The single biggest risk to manage is treating that core as "nearly done" — it is a funded, team-sized build. The fastest route to value is to run the strong part on a real project this month, not to keep building before anything has gone live.
