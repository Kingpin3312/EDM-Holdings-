Privacy

How we handle your details.

A plain-English summary of what we do with the information you share through this website.

When you submit an enquiry or download our capability statement, we collect the details you provide — typically your name, company, email and phone number — so that we can respond to you and follow up on your enquiry.

We use these details only to contact you about your enquiry and our services. We do not sell your information, and we don't share it with third parties except the service we use to deliver website form submissions to our inbox.

We keep enquiry details for as long as needed to deal with your request and any resulting work. If you'd like us to delete your details, or you have any question about your information, email us at enquiries@edmholdings.ae and we'll deal with it.

This summary is provided in good faith. For a formal data-protection policy tailored to the jurisdictions you operate in, take professional advice.
