Capability · Self-delivered

Aluminium & glazing.

Glazed partitioning defines the modern workplace — light, openness and a sense of space without giving up privacy or acoustic separation. We install it to the architect's drawings and finish it to exacting standards.

Talk to us All capabilities

What we deliver

Single and double-glazed partition systems, framed and frameless, with aluminium framing, doors and feature screens, acoustic-rated glass and manifestation to maximise light across the floorplate.

Glazing is delivered as part of the interior package, coordinated with the partitions, ceilings and joinery around it rather than handed to a separate trade.

How we work

Built into the sequence, not bolted on.

We set out the glazing lines from the architectural drawings, coordinate the head and base details with the surrounding drywall, and finish to the manifestation and acoustic specification called for.
