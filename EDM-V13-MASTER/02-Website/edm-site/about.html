About EDM Holdings

A specialist trade, self-delivered by people who have done it for forty years.

EDM Holdings is a specialist fit-out and drywall contractor built around a simple idea: the best commercial interiors are delivered by people who own every part of the work. Our people have been doing it since 1986.

Who we are

We partner with main contractors and principal contractors on commercial interiors across the UAE, Ireland, the UK and Australia. Wherever we work, the standard is the same: precise, accountable, and finished to the level the specification calls for.

Drywall and partitions are our core, and we self-deliver the trades around them — timber, glazing, joinery and finishes — so a main contractor can hand the inside of the building to one team rather than coordinating a chain of subcontractors.

The model

Self-delivered, start to finish.

Most fit-out packages are split across a string of trade subcontractors. Every split is an interface — and every interface is where programme, quality and accountability tend to slip. One company carries the interior from setting-out to handover, the same team that prices the work installs it, and the same standard runs through every trade.

Standards & assurance

Held to the standard the work demands.

Safety

An HSE-led culture on every site — method statements and risk assessments before work begins, permit-to-work where it applies, full inductions, and the discipline a live commercial site requires.

Quality

Work benchmarked at the outset and inspected as it proceeds, so issues are caught early rather than at handover. One team, one standard, carried straight through the package.

Programme

Realistic planning, sequencing and resourcing, with clear reporting and no surprises. We remove the inter-trade gaps that usually erode a programme — and say so early if anything threatens a date.

Accreditations, insurances, method statements and project references are available on request and issued to suit each client's pre-qualification requirements.

Leadership

The people who own the work.

One team, accountable end to end — led by people who have spent their careers in specialist interiors, on site as much as in the office.
