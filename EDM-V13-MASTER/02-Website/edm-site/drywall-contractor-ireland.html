Where we operate · Ireland

Fit-out and drywall contractor in Ireland.

Ireland — and Dublin in particular — runs one of Europe's busiest fit-out markets, from commercial offices to the life-sciences and data-centre work that has reshaped the sector. Across all of it the drywall package carries the fire and acoustic ratings the building depends on, and we build it to the certified system and Irish Building Regulations.

What we deliver

Metal stud partitions, dry lining, MF and suspended ceilings, and fire- and acoustic-rated systems, delivered into the commercial, life-sciences and data-centre buildings that drive Irish construction. We work from the certified detail, set out true, and finish to the level the specification calls for, with the rated build-ups documented for inspection.

Drywall is our core, and the timber, glazing, painting and joinery around it are ours to self-deliver too — so the inside of the building is handed over by one team.
