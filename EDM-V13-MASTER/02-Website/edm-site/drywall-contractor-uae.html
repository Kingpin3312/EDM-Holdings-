What we deliver

From Grade-A towers in Dubai and Abu Dhabi to hospitals, schools, hotels and government buildings the length of the country, the drywall package is the trade the rest of the fit-out is built on. We deliver the full scope — metal stud partitions, shaft walls, MF and suspended ceilings, and the fire- and acoustic-rated systems that have to satisfy Civil Defence and the consultant — across Dubai, Abu Dhabi, Sharjah, Ajman, Ras Al Khaimah, Fujairah and Umm Al Quwain.

Drywall is our core, and we self-deliver the timber, glazing, painting and joinery around it, so a main contractor anywhere in the country can hand the inside of the building to one accountable team. The trade licence, insurance and RAMS are in order, and a real person answers the phone.

Why UAE contractors use us

The UAE has no shortage of drywall subcontractors. What it has less of is a crew whose part of the programme does not slip. We give a date and build to it, price honestly from the drawings, build the ratings to a tested system, and hand over off the snag list rather than on it. On a fixed handover, that predictability is worth more than the cheapest rate.
