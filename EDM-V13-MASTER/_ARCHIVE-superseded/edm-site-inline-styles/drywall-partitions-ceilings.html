Drywall partitions & ceilings.

Internal walls and ceilings are the framework of every interior, and getting them right sets the standard for everything that follows. Drywall is our core trade — and it's where most fit-out programmes are won or lost.

Talk to us All capabilities

What we deliver

We install metal-stud partitions, shaft walls, dry lining, bulkheads and encasements, and MF, suspended and feature ceilings to fine tolerances — clean lines, true levels, and a substrate that's ready for finishes first time.

Fire- and acoustic-rated systems are built to the tested, certified detail and documented as they go in, so the rating stands up to inspection rather than failing at handover.

How we work

Built into the sequence, not bolted on.

We set out from the structural grid and the reflected ceiling plan, check the build-up against the fire and acoustic schedules before we frame, and sequence the floor so the trades that follow have a clean, accurate base. Components are ordered against the specification rather than substituted on site.
