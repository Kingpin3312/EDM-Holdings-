What we deliver

Commercial office fit-out is the heart of the London market, and the drywall package sits at the centre of it: metal stud partitions, dry lining, MF and suspended ceilings, and the fire- and acoustic-rated systems a London building depends on. We build to the certified system and the relevant British Standard, set out from the grid and the reflected ceiling plan, and finish to the level the specification calls for — taped through to a Level-5 finish where critical lighting demands it.

Drywall is our core, and we self-deliver the timber, glazing, painting and joinery around it, so a main contractor gets one accountable team for the interior rather than a chain of subcontractors to coordinate.

How we work in London

We mobilise to the main contractor's site rules, build the rated systems to satisfy Building Control and the fire strategy, and keep the build-up documented for inspection.
