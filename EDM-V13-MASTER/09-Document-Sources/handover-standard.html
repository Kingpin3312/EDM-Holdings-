THE HANDOVER
STANDARD

Evidence, not promises.

The riskiest moment in any drywall package is the day a wall gets boarded over. Once it is closed, nobody can see what is behind it, and years later somebody has to prove what was built. We decided that proof should be part of the product.

How it works on our sites
1

Every wall has a number

Our takeoff gives each run a reference at tender stage — the same reference it keeps through construction and into the handover file. The wall we priced, the wall we built and the wall we are claiming for are provably the same wall.

2

Every stage is inspected and signed

Setting out, framing, services closed in, insulation, each layer of board, fire-stopping, decoration — checked in sequence, dated and initialled by the supervisor who looked at it.

3

Photographed before it disappears

No wall is boarded over without a photograph of the services inside it, and no fire-stopping is signed off without a picture of the seal. The record will not accept a pass without the photograph.

4

Handed over as a pack, not a promise

At completion, or at each zone where the works are zoned, you receive the full record: every wall, every check, every photograph, as one document your team can file, audit and rely on.

Why it matters to a main contractor

Fire-safety regulation increasingly expects a traceable thread of building information, and interior walls are where that thread most often breaks. Our records give you the evidence trail for our package: fewer opening-up exercises, faster sign-off, and an answer on file years later. Failed checks stay on the record beside the passes — that is what makes a pass worth something.

What it costs you
Nothing. It is how we work. The system is ours, built in-house, and travels with every EDM package as standard. All we ask is that the inspection and test plan and the benchmark standard are agreed early, so the record measures the right things against a standard both parties have signed.
