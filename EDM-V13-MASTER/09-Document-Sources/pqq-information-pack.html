Before this pack goes out
Every bracketed field is filled. A pack with brackets still in it tells a buyer more about how you run a job than the answers do.
The trade licence is attached and in date, and the licensed activities cover everything in the Scope row.
Insurance certificates are attached and none expires inside the tender period.
Named references have given consent. Where they have not, the row says "on request".
Turnover matches the accounts being supplied with it.

Keep one master copy of this pack and update it on every renewal. A stale insurance date is the most common reason a prequalification comes back.
