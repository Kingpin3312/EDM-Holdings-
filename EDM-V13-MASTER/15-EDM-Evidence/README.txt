EDM EVIDENCE — quality & fire-stopping records
==============================================

What it is
----------
The other half of EDM Measure. Measure numbers the walls and prices them;
Evidence proves they were built right. Every wall gets an inspection
record — eight stages, signed and dated — and the two stages that matter
most (services closed in, fire-stopping) will not accept a pass without a
photograph. At the end, one button prints the branded handover pack.

How to use it
-------------
1. Open index.html in any modern browser. Works on a phone — the photo
   button opens the camera on site.
2. Import from EDM Measure: open the saved .edm.json takeoff and the wall
   register arrives with the same references. Or add walls by hand.
3. Work each wall through its stages: Pass / Fail / N-A, initials, note.
   Attach the photo before boarding and at fire-stopping — the record
   insists on it.
4. Save keeps everything (photos included) in one file.
5. Print handover pack produces the client-facing document: cover summary,
   then one record per wall with its photographs. Use your browser's
   "Save as PDF" to issue it.

Straight talk
-------------
- Failed checks stay on the record next to the passes. That is the point.
- Works fully offline — nothing leaves the device until you share the file.
- Pair it with the one-pager in 03-Corporate-Documents
  (EDM-Handover-Standard.pdf) when tendering: that page tells the client
  this system exists; this tool is the proof.
